## 🌟 Overview

This dashboard turns raw coffee shop transactions into clear, visual answers:

- 🗓 **When** does revenue grow or slow down?
- 🏙 **Where** are the best-performing stores?
- 🕘 **What hours** bring the biggest rush?
- 🥐 **Which products** earn the most?

## 🎮 How to Interact

| 👆 Action | ✨ Result |
|---|---|
| Click a **month** button | Filters the whole dashboard to that month |
| Click a **product category** button | Updates every chart for that category |
| Hold **Ctrl** and click | Selects multiple options at once |
| Click the **filter icon** in a slicer | Clears the selection |

> [!TIP]
> Select only **Coffee** and **June** to see your top category in your best month.

## 🔍 Key Insights

| | Insight |
|:---:|---|
| 📈 | **Strong growth:** Monthly revenue climbs from about 81.7K in January to about 166.5K in June. |
| ⚖ | **Balanced stores:** All three locations earn similar revenue, with Lower Manhattan slightly ahead. |
| 🌅 | **Morning rush:** Sales peak around 10 AM (about 73.2K) and stay strong from 8 to 10 AM. |
| 🏆 | **Star category:** One product category reaches about 224K, far above the rest. |
| 🍵 | **Top product type:** Brewed Chai tea leads with about 63.8K. |
